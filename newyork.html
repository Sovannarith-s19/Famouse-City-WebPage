New York

Skyscrapers, big parks and a new neighbourhood on every subway stop.

CountryUnited States
Best timeApr – Jun, Sep – Nov
LanguageEnglish
CurrencyUS dollar ($)

Places you should not miss

Start with these four. They cover the classic sights and a few quieter corners.

Statue of Liberty

Take the ferry from Battery Park and look back at the skyline.

Central Park

Rent a bike, row a boat or just find a bench and watch the city pass.

Times Square

Loud, bright and crowded. Visit once at night and keep moving.

Brooklyn Bridge

Walk across at golden hour for views of Manhattan and the harbour.

A three-day plan

  1. Day 1: DowntownStatue of Liberty ferry, 9/11 Memorial, Brooklyn Bridge walk.
  2. Day 2: MidtownEmpire State Building, Grand Central, Times Square after dark.
  3. Day 3: Parks and museumsCentral Park, the Met, then dinner in Greenwich Village.

What to eat

Pizza slice

A wide, foldable slice for a few dollars. Eat it standing up.

Bagel with cream cheese

The classic New York breakfast, fresh from a corner shop.

Deli sandwich

Towering pastrami on rye. Share one between two people.
