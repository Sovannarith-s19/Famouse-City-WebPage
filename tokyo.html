Tokyo

Neon crossings, quiet shrines and the best train system on earth.

CountryJapan
Best timeMar – May, Oct – Nov
LanguageJapanese
CurrencyYen (¥)

Places you should not miss

Start with these four. They cover the classic sights and a few quieter corners.

Shibuya Crossing

Thousands cross at once. Watch from the cafés above for the best view.

Senso-ji Temple

Tokyo’s oldest temple, reached through a lively market street in Asakusa.

Meiji Shrine

A forest path in the middle of the city, leading to a calm Shinto shrine.

Akihabara

Arcades, anime shops and electronics in every direction.

A three-day plan

  1. Day 1: Modern TokyoShibuya, Harajuku, then Shinjuku’s lights at night.
  2. Day 2: Old TokyoSenso-ji, Ueno Park and a stroll along the Sumida River.
  3. Day 3: Pop cultureAkihabara in the morning, teamLab or a sumo-town lunch in Ryogoku.

What to eat

Sushi

Order at a counter or conveyor-belt shop for fresh fish at fair prices.

Ramen

Rich noodle soup. Many shops use a ticket machine at the door.

Convenience-store snacks

Onigiri rice balls and egg sandwiches are better than they sound.
