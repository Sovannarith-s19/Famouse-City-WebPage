Paris

The city of light, wide boulevards and long café afternoons.

CountryFrance
Best timeApr – Jun, Sep – Oct
LanguageFrench
CurrencyEuro (€)

Places you should not miss

Start with these four. They cover the classic sights and a few quieter corners.

Eiffel Tower

Go up at sunset, then watch it sparkle on the hour after dark.

Louvre Museum

Home of the Mona Lisa. Book a timed ticket and pick two wings, not ten.

Montmartre

Hilltop streets, artists’ squares and the white dome of Sacré-Cœur.

Seine River

Walk the quays or take an evening boat to see the bridges lit up.

A three-day plan

  1. Day 1: IconsEiffel Tower, Champ de Mars picnic, evening Seine cruise.
  2. Day 2: Art and historyLouvre in the morning, Tuileries Garden, Notre-Dame area.
  3. Day 3: Village ParisMontmartre at breakfast, Canal Saint-Martin, dinner in Le Marais.

What to eat

Croissants

Buy one warm from a neighbourhood boulangerie before 9am.

Croque monsieur

A toasted ham and cheese sandwich. Simple, filling, everywhere.

Crêpes

Sweet or savoury, from a street stand or a proper crêperie.
